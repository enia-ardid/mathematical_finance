# Publication and distribution

The [manuscript](../paper/main.pdf) develops a conditional-variance model with fractional memory, persistent asymmetric responses and graph diffusion. Its equations, assumptions and results are documented alongside the code. The [related-work comparison](related_work.md) explains its relationship to existing volatility models. The earlier simulation and price-forecast workflows remain available with their own documented scope.

The numerical studies use retrospective adjusted-price snapshots. Their protocols, source hashes, aggregate results and tests are included; raw prices and detailed origin-level records are retained locally and are not redistributed. The [data guide](data.md) describes sources, formats and vintage limitations. New downloads may differ from the retained snapshots.

The [LaTeX package](../paper/latex-source.zip) includes the manuscript dependencies and aggregate records. The [arXiv source package](../paper/arxiv-source.zip) includes only the files needed to compile the paper. Build and reproduction instructions are in the [paper guide](../paper/README.md). No arXiv or journal submission has been performed.

Use [CITATION.cff](../CITATION.cff) and identify the Git commit when citing the project. No reuse license has been selected. Repository visibility does not itself grant a software or manuscript license.

Raw CSVs, local environments, caches and execution artifacts are excluded from the repository. The maintained notebook contains executed synthetic examples. The beta notebook documents the earlier implementation and is not used for the manuscript results.
